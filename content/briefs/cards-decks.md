# Deck assignments (one author per file)

| file | decks | minimum | what to cover |
|---|---|---|---|
| `app/data/cards-terms.js` | `terms` (≥ 110) + `abbr` (≥ 55) | 165 | **terms**: the most exam-relevant glossary terms of Tracks B–E (and the paper-specific names: Tier, pan-hallmark, CHiLL1, GCAWKR, DECKO, pgRNA, libDECKO-NSCLC1 …): "What is X?" with a one-line answer in your own words (use the glossary `short` as a guide, but rewrite so the card stands alone). **abbr**: every abbreviation the paper uses that the learner must know (TSS, ASO, MOI, FDR, DEG, NSCLC, LUAD, CRISPR, gDNA, qRT-PCR, FACS, GFP, PDX, TCGA, CCLE, PCAWG, GEO, ROC, AUC, CPM, TPM, FPKM, GSEA, KEGG, MSigDB, RRA, EBM, HMP, TPP, ORF, PCG, lncRNA, mRNA, miRNA, bp, kb, STR, FBS, PBS, DAPI, NGS, RSEM, FISH, LNA, CFSE, EMT, MAPK, mTORC1 … check `node tools/q.mjs term`): back = what the letters stand for + one-line meaning. |
| `app/data/cards-why.js` | `why` (≥ 50) | 50 | Design-logic cards: "Why delete the TSS (not the whole gene)?", "Why paired guides?", "Why MOI 0.3?", "Why ≥ coverage?", "Why puromycin?", "Why two NSCLC cell lines?", "Why also test normal lung cells?", "Why two ASOs per target?", "Why a non-targeting ASO?", "Why ORF-targeting and TSS-targeting guides as controls?", "Why neutral/positive controls?", "Why log2?", "Why FDR instead of raw P?", "Why replicate screens?", "Why 3D and PDX validation?", "Why rescue?", "Why fractionate cells / RNA-FISH?", "Why normalise to a housekeeping gene?", "Why a low (IC20-type) cisplatin dose?" … — only reasons the paper states or that are general knowledge (mark those `kind:'background'`). Each back: the reason + what would go wrong otherwise. Read the Methods and Results paragraphs for each. |
| `app/data/cards-numbers.js` | `numbers` (≥ 60) | 60 | Key paper numbers *as questions*: counts in the funnel (831, 998, 80, 9, 5, 2), library size and guides per target, MOI, coverage, time points, cell numbers, doses, knockdown thresholds, FDR/fold-change cut-offs, replicate numbers, sample sizes, public-data cohort sizes, specific P/effect sizes quoted in the text, etc. Front hides the number ("How many …?", "What cut-off …?"), back gives it with its meaning. When the paper gives conflicting numbers for the same thing, make a card that states both (`front`: "Why do 12,000 and 1,200 both appear …?"-style) — only if the conflict is real (verify). |
| `app/data/cards-figures.js` | `figures` (≥ 60) | 60 | **Every one of the 49 panels at least once** (`img:'fig4C'`; front "What does this panel show?" / "What is plotted on the axes, and what is the main take-home?" / "What does the control show?"), plus whole figures (`img:'fig2'`) and the graphical abstract (`img:'ga'`): "What question does Figure 3 answer?", plus 8–12 "how to read this chart" cards for the trickiest panels. Use `lesson:'G-<unit that cites the panel>'` (`node tools/q.mjs panel fig4C` shows the citing paragraphs). Back ≤ 35 words with the main numbers (they must be in the panel legend, the panel's figure text `FIGTEXT`, or the citing paragraphs; `source:['fig4C','res-integration-p3']`). |
| `app/data/cards-concepts.js` | `concepts` (≥ 70) | 70 | Results, logic, methods and statistics: "What are dropout and positive selection?", "What does the funnel 831 → 80 → 9 → 5 → 2 represent?", "What did the paper conclude about CHiLL1 / GCAWKR (mode of action)?", "What does a CRISPR deletion remove vs an ASO?", "How is a hit defined?", "What is FDR?", "Which test fits …?", "What is the difference between 2D, 3D and PDX models?", "What does the paper admit as limitations?", "What is the patent/COI declaration?", "What does Welch's t test do?" … Mix `background` (Tracks B–F knowledge) and `paper` (with sources) cards. |
